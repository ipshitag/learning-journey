## 2025-02-14
- **Great Fire of 1657 (Great Fire of Meireki) 🔥**: Occurred in Edo (Tokyo), devastating much of the city. It prompted significant urban rebuilding and fire prevention measures, shaping the city's layout that endures today.
- **Cucumber Lemon Tea 🍋** - Made by infusing cucumber and lemon in water. It helps the body by providing hydration, aiding detoxification, and supporting weight loss.
- **Bumblebee bat 🦇** - Also known has Kitty's hog nosed bat, is the smallest bat in the world. It is found in Myanmar and Thailand.
