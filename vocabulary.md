# Vocabulary

## 2025-02-15
### Promulgate
- **Meaning:** to spread an idea or belief widely
- **Example:** The teacher promulgates kindness in all of their classes.
- **Personal Connection:** Influencers and media houses promulgated Kumbh 2025 as an event not to be missed, causing nationwide FOMO.

## 2025-02-14
### Verdant
- **Meaning:** green with plant life, generally green-coloured.
- **Example Sentence:** "I was enchanted by the lush,verdant landscape."
- **Personal Connection:** "Sawantwadi's verdant landscape evokes a feeling of peace."

## 2025-02-13
### Inveterate
- **Meaning:** deeply ingrained and unlikely to change
- **Example Sentence:** "I have an inveterate habit of trusting everyone I meet."
- **Personal Connection:** "I’m an inveterate night owl, somehow managing to power through my night shifts, even when it feels like I might doze off any second."
